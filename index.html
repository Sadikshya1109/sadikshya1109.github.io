ABOUT ME & RESEARCH INTERESTS

Hello, I'm Sadikshya.

I explore how hydrological modelling and interpretable machine learning can help us better understand water in complex mountain regions.

I'm a civil engineer with an MSc in Hydropower Engineering from the Institute of Engineering, Pulchowk Campus, Nepal. My work connects physical models, data-driven methods and practical questions in hydropower and water-resource management.
